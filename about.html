I've always known that I wanted a life spent creating. To build living, breathing, worlds with my own two hands—using whatever medium I can get my grubby little hands on.
Games, VR, 3D models, animation, digital art, oil pastels, colored pencils. Anything! I've surely gone through all the phases!
Alas, it is truly one of the most precarious times to be an artist. Will I even be relevant when worlds and pictures and poetry can be made faster than I can blink? If creation takes so long to do, can I make a living? Will I be sought after? What security is there?
Yet still, when I create things, I feel so wholly Me. It simply calls to me. I am restless if I cannot write or draw—I cannot eat, I cannot sleep, I am enamored. I want to be absorbed in this feeling. In the tumolt of creating.
Yes. I want to use the time I have now to create things that matter. Things that will last beyond me and inspire the best in people.
Create with me, something that matters.
 Together.
