Kayamidad is a student-run thesis project designed to make disaster preparedness fun, accessible, and engaging for gradeschool teachers and students.
I established the brand direction and core visual identity while working collaboratively to produce the educational print materials for the mixed media kit, including the Bidasaster card game, booklet, and guide pamphlet.

This is Kuya Kiko! Our Kayamidad mascot with a "Kaya natin yan!" attitude. He plays the 'kuya' role as someone who students and guardians can connect with as he guides students through the activities, games, and lessons.

The main educational component of the project is the Kayamidad Disaster Preparedness Handbook. It's filled with lessons, pictures, puzzles, and illustrations dedicated to helping readers identify different natural disasters and what they can do before, during, and after disaster strikes.

Kayamidad's visual identity is built on putting learning and clarity first. Using core colors, simple shapes, and easy-to-understand language, we make learning more accessible for the students, making them way more excited to engage!
