Kayamidad is a student-run project designed to make disaster preparedness fun, accessible, and engaging for gradeschool teachers and students. I established the brand direction and core visual identity while working collaboratively to produce the educational print materials for the mixed media kit, including the Bidasaster card game, booklet, and guide pamphlet.

This is Kuya Kiko! Our Kayamidad mascot with a "Kaya natin yan!" attitude. He plays a role in being someone that students and guardians can connect with as he guides students through the activities, games, and lessons.
